Upload Phim

A private desktop tool that helps a small team upload and schedule videos to their own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads videos from your computer to YouTube channels you own or manage, using the official YouTube Data API.
  • Lets you queue many videos, track upload progress, pause, resume, retry and reorder uploads.
  • Lets you schedule upload times and YouTube publish times, set titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and playlists.

How sign-in works

You connect each YouTube channel by signing in with Google and granting permission to upload videos. The tool runs on your own computer. Access tokens are stored only on that computer and are never sent to us or to any third party.

Google API usage

Upload Phim uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.
